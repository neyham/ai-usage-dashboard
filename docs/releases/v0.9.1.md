## AI Usage Dashboard v0.9.1

OpenCode Go support, one X toggle for Grok and Cursor, and characters that emerge from the corners of wide cards.

### Highlights

- **OpenCode Go.** A new card shows the 5-hour, weekly, and monthly subscription windows with reset times, for Go and Go Plus. The key is read, read-only, from OpenCode's own login: `opencode.db` (OpenCode 2.x) or `auth.json` (1.x) under `~/.local/share/opencode` on every platform, then `OPENCODE_API_KEY`. Only the percentages the server reports are shown. Go and Go Plus set limits per model, so local cost history is not used to estimate quota, and the two plans are not told apart. Disabled by default; enable it in Display Settings.
- **One X toggle.** Display Settings now has a single "X · Grok + Cursor" switch. A switched X Subscription seat shows one X card and no longer reads Cursor's own feed. An unswitched seat keeps separate Grok and Cursor cards and hides whichever side has no local login. The X ring is now one ring split by product, matching the bar.
- **Corner characters.** On wide cards, the character emerges from a lower corner of the card and is cropped by the card's own edge. All six characters have new corner poses in every mood. OpenCode Go has its own character; the former Cursor character was remade for it, and unswitched Cursor cards now use the X (Grok) character.
- **Seven-card layout.** With every provider on an unswitched seat, the board shows four cards over three.

### Fixes

- Classic EVA skin: cards with four usage rows (Antigravity's two groups, Cursor's breakdown) no longer overlap the panel header and status footer.
- IP skin: the floating clock and buttons no longer cover card titles. The five-card layout with three cards on top placed the clock over the middle card's title; cards under the clock or buttons now keep their titles clear, and long plan labels are truncated.
- A spent quota shows a LIMIT chip instead of ERROR.

### Installation

- **Windows x64:** NSIS `.exe` or MSI. The WinGet community index may lag this release.
- **Linux x64:** DEB or AppImage. AppImage extract-and-run is an alternative when FUSE is unavailable.
- **macOS Apple Silicon:** DMG. No Intel macOS installer is included.

The `install-linux.sh` and `install-macos.sh` helpers are also attached. Verify downloads against `AI-Usage-Dashboard_0.9.1_SHA256SUMS.txt`.

### Compatibility and limits

- OpenCode Go reads the usage endpoint `opencode.ai/zen/go/v1/usage`, which is not a documented public API. Incompatible responses fail closed to last-known-good data.
- The 960×540 six-card EVA layout can still overlap on two-row cards.
- The release workflow tests upgrades from the published 0.9.0 packages, startup with synthetic data, repeat install or repair, and executable removal. Renderer interaction tests run separately in Chromium.
- Installers remain unsigned/not notarized; system trust prompts are not covered by CI.
